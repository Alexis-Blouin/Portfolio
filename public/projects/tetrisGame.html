OpenGL 3D Tetris Game

Another project I accomplished during my semester in Norway is a 3D Tetris Game. I made this with C++ and OpenGL as a final assessment for a class. I constructed abstractions to the rendering pipeline components such as shader, camera, VAO, VBO, etc.

While it was my first time with graphic development, I had a lot of fun and now understand better how things are displayed on a screen.
