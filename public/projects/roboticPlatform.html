Robotic Platform With Android App

During a course work, we had the opportunity to design a project from scratch. The only constraint were tha use of an Android app and a robotic device. Me and my team came with the idea of a mobile platform with an arm mounted on top of it. It would be controlled by the Android application. There was 3 main challenges to this project: the Android application, the robotic arm and the mobile platform. I was assigned the robotic arm with the team management. One teammate was on the mobile platform and the last one worked on the Android app.

The biggest challenges we faces was how to power so many electric motors. The little Arduino and Raspberry Pi were not designed to deliver such power and we ended up burning one battery. Then we were more careful with connections and we managed to make it work. I work mainly on the Arduino program that could control the robotic arm. The only problem I had was physical: the motors were not perfectly fit and I had to design a piece the allow the proper connection.
